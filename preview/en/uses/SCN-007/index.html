The problem

Complaint systems are designed to close individual tickets. When dozens of people report similar symptoms, failures or observations, the institution may still see unrelated files rather than one emerging systemic event.

What breaks

  • Fragmentation
  • Citizen Signal Ignored
  • Signal Dilution
  • Pattern Detection Failure

Who holds part of the picture

  • frontline practitioners
  • domain experts
  • decision-makers
  • affected people

With Koali

Koali links reports without erasing their individuality. Semantic reconciliation can cluster similar signals, preserve provenance, expose geographic or temporal patterns and open an escalation case when governed thresholds are met.

Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

What success looks like

  • The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
  • Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
  • The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.

Real-world parallels
