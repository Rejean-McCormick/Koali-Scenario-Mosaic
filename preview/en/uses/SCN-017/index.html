The problem

Institutions can collect consultation comments without making local knowledge part of the evidence model. Technical categories may fail to represent seasonal patterns, cultural meanings, informal infrastructure or lived constraints.

With Koali

Koali represents different knowledge types explicitly, preserving source authority and uncertainty. Semantic reconciliation lets local concepts and technical categories interact without silently forcing one into the other.

Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

What success looks like

  • The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
  • Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
  • The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.
