The problem

Security sees indicators of compromise; IT sees broken systems; operations sees unavailable services; legal sees obligations; communications sees public risk; executives need choices. If those streams separate, decisions lag or contradict one another.

What breaks

With Koali

Koali holds the incident as a governed timeline: facts versus hypotheses, affected services, owners, containment decisions, dependencies, external requests, communications and recovery gates. The post-incident record is produced by the response itself.

Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

What success looks like

  • The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
  • Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
  • The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.
