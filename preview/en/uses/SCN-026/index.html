The problem

A city, fund, hospital or ministry must choose among options that trade cost, safety, access, resilience, speed and equity. A single ranking hides the fact that different criteria encode different priorities.

What breaks

  • Criterion Ambiguity
  • Hidden Tradeoff
  • Opaque Weighting
  • Expertise Isolation

Who holds part of the picture

  • frontline practitioners
  • domain experts
  • decision-makers
  • affected people

With Koali

Koali makes options, criteria, evidence and weighting assumptions visible. Multiple governed readings can coexist instead of pretending there is one neutral score.

Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

What success looks like

  • The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
  • Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
  • The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.
