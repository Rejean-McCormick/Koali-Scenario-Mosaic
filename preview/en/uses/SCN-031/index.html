The problem

An organization improves its average wait time, service completion or health outcome. The dashboard turns green. Yet one subgroup experiences worse outcomes, or rare severe failures remain invisible inside the aggregate.

What breaks

  • Aggregation Bias
  • Visibility Failure
  • Metric Fixation
  • Citizen Signal Ignored

Who holds part of the picture

  • public health
  • clinicians
  • community organizations
  • affected people

With Koali

Koali keeps aggregate indicators linked to disaggregated evidence, exceptions and citizen signals. A positive overall trend does not automatically close an unresolved high-severity pattern.

Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

What success looks like

  • The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
  • Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
  • The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.
