The problem

A major transformation produces thousands of documents and meetings. Requirements shift, costs rise, testing reveals new risks and suppliers propose changes. Years later, an auditor can find the decision but not the full chain of evidence and trade-offs that produced it.

What breaks

  • Opaque Decision
  • Context Loss
  • Fragmentation
  • Institutional Dependency

Who holds part of the picture

  • frontline practitioners
  • domain experts
  • decision-makers
  • affected people

With Koali

Koali binds major decisions to the evidence, options, dissent, cost assumptions, risks, approvals and downstream work they created. The project becomes a decision graph rather than a pile of files.

Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

What success looks like

  • The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
  • Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
  • The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.
