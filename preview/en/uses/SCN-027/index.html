The problem

A consultation collects thousands of submissions. Staff summarize them; a decision is made somewhere else; participants cannot see how their contribution affected the result. Even when a good proposal is accepted, there may be no operational owner.

What breaks

  • Unclosed Loop
  • Participation Without Power
  • Memory Failure
  • Opaque Decision

Who holds part of the picture

  • citizens
  • experts
  • decision-makers
  • affected groups

With Koali

Koali connects contribution to structured arguments, options, decision, mandate, tasks, responsible roles, evidence of completion and later evaluation. Participants can see the path without requiring every suggestion to be accepted.

Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

What success looks like

  • The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
  • Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
  • The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.
