The problem

A local event is real: a cold day, flood, drought or wildfire. The error is using one observation as though it directly establishes or refutes a long-term global trend.

What breaks

  • Anecdote Overweight
  • Scale Confusion
  • Noise Amplification
  • Context Loss

Who holds part of the picture

  • frontline practitioners
  • domain experts
  • decision-makers
  • affected people

With Koali

Koali links claims to the appropriate scale, time horizon and evidence class. A local observation can remain true without being misused as proof about a different statistical question.

Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

What success looks like

  • The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
  • Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
  • The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.
