The problem

An archive digitizes songs, stories, images or knowledge. The files survive, but creator, community authority, consent, restrictions, sacred context and permitted uses may not travel with them. Search, remix and AI systems then treat culture as context-free content.

What breaks

  • Provenance Loss
  • Rights Detachment
  • Context Loss
  • Power Asymmetry

Who holds part of the picture

  • creators

With Koali

Koali keeps cultural objects connected to provenance, rights, authority, consent, access conditions, version history and contested interpretations. Discovery does not require stripping the object from its governing relationships.

Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

What success looks like

  • The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
  • Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
  • The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.
