# Publish the Trade Before the Last Master Leaves.

> Skills that live only in hands and stories can vanish faster than institutions realize.

## The problem

Some work cannot be learned from a manual: diagnosing a vibration, reading ice, handling an old machine, restoring a material, recognizing when a process is almost wrong. If masters retire faster than apprentices absorb that judgment, capability disappears.

## What breaks

- Tacit Knowledge Loss
- Knowledge Attrition
- Single Point Of Failure
- Training Gap

## Who holds part of the picture

- creators
- communities
- archives
- users

## With Koali

Koali links demonstrations, cases, explanations, conditions, failures and mentor commentary into a living practice library. Documentation complements apprenticeship rather than pretending to replace it.

## Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

## What success looks like

- The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
- Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
- The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.

## Real-world parallels

### IAEA — Nuclear Knowledge Management

The IAEA treats retirement and loss of critical knowledge as explicit organizational risks requiring succession planning and knowledge-loss assessment.

**Relation to scenario:** `DIRECT`  
**Source:** https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/Publications/PDF/Pub1724_web.pdf

### UNESCO Recommendation on Open Educational Resources

UNESCO promotes open access, reuse, adaptation and redistribution of teaching and learning materials.

**Relation to scenario:** `RELATED PATTERN`  
**Source:** https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-open-educational-resources-oer

## Boundary

The documented cases illustrate analogous failure mechanisms. They do not establish that Koali would have prevented the event or guaranteed a different outcome.
