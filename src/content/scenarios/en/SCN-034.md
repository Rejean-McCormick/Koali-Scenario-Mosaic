# Nobody Knows Why the Legacy System Works This Way.

> A system can remain operational long after the decisions, people and documentation that made it understandable are gone.

## The problem

A decades-old system contains patches, exceptions and hidden dependencies. Documentation says what modules do, but not why a rule exists or which historical failure created it. Staff become afraid to change anything.

## What breaks

- Knowledge Attrition
- Legacy Dependency
- Context Loss
- Single Point Of Failure

## Who holds part of the picture

- frontline practitioners
- domain experts
- decision-makers
- affected people

## With Koali

Koali links technical components to decisions, incidents, policy rules, known exceptions and knowledge holders. Modernization teams can reconstruct why before removing what merely looks unnecessary.

## Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

## What success looks like

- The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
- Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
- The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.

## Real-world parallels

### U.S. GAO — Critical Legacy Systems

GAO identified critical federal legacy systems 8 to 51 years old, including outdated languages, unsupported technology and known vulnerabilities.

**Relation to scenario:** `DIRECT`  
**Source:** https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-19-471

### Auditor General of Canada — Phoenix example

The Auditor General reiterated that Phoenix executives prioritized schedule and budget over critical elements and did not provide senior officials a complete picture of project risks.

**Relation to scenario:** `STRONG ANALOGUE`  
**Source:** https://www.oag-bvg.gc.ca/internet/docs/parl_oag_202310_07_e.pdf

## Boundary

The documented cases illustrate analogous failure mechanisms. They do not establish that Koali would have prevented the event or guaranteed a different outcome.
