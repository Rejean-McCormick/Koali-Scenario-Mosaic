# Twenty Thousand People Participate. Then Nothing Happens.

> Participation without a governed path to decision and execution can create engagement while destroying trust.

## The problem

A consultation collects thousands of submissions. Staff summarize them; a decision is made somewhere else; participants cannot see how their contribution affected the result. Even when a good proposal is accepted, there may be no operational owner.

## What breaks

- Unclosed Loop
- Participation Without Power
- Memory Failure
- Opaque Decision

## Who holds part of the picture

- citizens
- experts
- decision-makers
- affected groups

## With Koali

Koali connects contribution to structured arguments, options, decision, mandate, tasks, responsible roles, evidence of completion and later evaluation. Participants can see the path without requiring every suggestion to be accepted.

## Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

## What success looks like

- The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
- Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
- The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.

## Real-world parallels

### Grenfell Tower Inquiry — Phase 2 Overview

The Inquiry found that residents’ concerns were repeatedly marginalized and earlier independent recommendations had produced little visible change.

**Relation to scenario:** `STRONG ANALOGUE`  
**Source:** https://www.grenfelltowerinquiry.org.uk/report/phase-2/overview.html

### FDA — History of the agency’s role in HIV/AIDS

The FDA documents how AIDS activists pushed regulators toward faster access and more direct patient participation in policy and advisory processes.

**Relation to scenario:** `REAL POSITIVE ANALOGUE`  
**Source:** https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/fda-history-exhibits/history-fdas-role-preventing-spread-hivaids

## Boundary

The documented cases illustrate analogous failure mechanisms. They do not establish that Koali would have prevented the event or guaranteed a different outcome.
