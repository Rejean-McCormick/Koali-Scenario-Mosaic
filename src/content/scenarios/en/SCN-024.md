# Experts Should Inform the Decision, Not Own It.

> A society needs expertise without turning expertise into unaccountable sovereignty.

## The problem

Complex decisions mix facts, forecasts, values and distributional choices. Pure majoritarianism can ignore specialized knowledge; pure expert rule can ignore legitimacy and lived consequences.

## What breaks

- Expertise Mismatch
- Legitimacy Gap
- Opaque Weighting
- False Balance

## Who holds part of the picture

- citizens
- experts
- decision-makers
- affected groups

## With Koali

Koali can display an unweighted democratic reading alongside transparent advisory readings based on domain-bounded expertise. It records where advice and final decision diverge and preserves the reasons.

## Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

## What success looks like

- The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
- Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
- The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.
