# Residents Keep Warning. The Institution Stops Hearing.

> When persistent complainants are classified as a nuisance, the organization can erase the feedback channel that reveals systemic risk.

## The problem

Residents repeatedly report safety or service problems. Each complaint may be processed, but the institution gradually interprets frequent complainants as difficult rather than treating persistence as possible evidence of unresolved systemic failure.

## What breaks

- Citizen Signal Ignored
- Power Asymmetry
- Fragmentation
- Memory Failure

## Who holds part of the picture

- frontline practitioners
- domain experts
- decision-makers
- affected people

## With Koali

Koali preserves complaints as longitudinal signals, links them to inspections and corrective work, and keeps unresolved risk open. It separates the tone or popularity of a complainant from the evidentiary status of the issue.

## Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

## What success looks like

- The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
- Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
- The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.

## Real-world parallels

### Grenfell Tower Inquiry — Phase 2 Overview

The Inquiry found that residents’ concerns were repeatedly marginalized and earlier independent recommendations had produced little visible change.

**Relation to scenario:** `DIRECT`  
**Source:** https://www.grenfelltowerinquiry.org.uk/report/phase-2/overview.html

### EPA OIG — Flint Water Crisis

EPA’s inspector general reported 87 citizen contacts, including 30 about lead, without a system that used complaint volume as a systemic signal.

**Relation to scenario:** `STRONG ANALOGUE`  
**Source:** https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=P1015LRE.TXT

## Boundary

The documented cases illustrate analogous failure mechanisms. They do not establish that Koali would have prevented the event or guaranteed a different outcome.
