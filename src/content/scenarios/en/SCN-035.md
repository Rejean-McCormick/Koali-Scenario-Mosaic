# Digitized Culture Without Context Becomes Raw Material.

> Preservation without provenance, authority and rights can turn living culture into extractable data.

## The problem

An archive digitizes songs, stories, images or knowledge. The files survive, but creator, community authority, consent, restrictions, sacred context and permitted uses may not travel with them. Search, remix and AI systems then treat culture as context-free content.

## What breaks

- Provenance Loss
- Rights Detachment
- Context Loss
- Power Asymmetry

## Who holds part of the picture

- creators
- communities
- archives
- users

## With Koali

Koali keeps cultural objects connected to provenance, rights, authority, consent, access conditions, version history and contested interpretations. Discovery does not require stripping the object from its governing relationships.

## Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

## What success looks like

- The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
- Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
- The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.

## Real-world parallels

### Local Contexts — Traditional Knowledge Labels

Traditional Knowledge Labels attach community-specific authority, provenance and conditions of use to digital cultural materials.

**Relation to scenario:** `REAL POSITIVE ANALOGUE`  
**Source:** https://localcontexts.org/labels/traditional-knowledge-labels/

### WIPO — IP and Museums, Libraries and Archives

WIPO notes that digitization of traditional cultural expressions can create misuse or misappropriation risks if rights and context are not preserved.

**Relation to scenario:** `DIRECT`  
**Source:** https://www.wipo.int/en/web/traditional-knowledge/resources/museums

## Boundary

The documented cases illustrate analogous failure mechanisms. They do not establish that Koali would have prevented the event or guaranteed a different outcome.
