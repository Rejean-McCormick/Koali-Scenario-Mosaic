# The Miracle Cure Goes Viral Before the Trials Finish.

> Early plausibility is not the same thing as demonstrated benefit.

## The problem

A treatment has a plausible mechanism, laboratory evidence or an early uncontrolled study. Public attention converts worth testing into works. By the time randomized evidence arrives, identities and political narratives may already be attached to the claim.

## What breaks

- Noise Amplification
- Premature Conclusion
- Provenance Loss
- False Certainty

## Who holds part of the picture

- frontline practitioners
- domain experts
- decision-makers
- affected people

## With Koali

Koali versions the claim over time: hypothesis, early evidence, active trials, interim uncertainty, later synthesis. Public explanations remain attached to the evidence state that was valid when they were published.

## Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

## What success looks like

- The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
- Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
- The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.

## Real-world parallels

### WHO — COVID-19: Hydroxychloroquine

WHO’s synthesis of randomized trials found no mortality or ventilation benefit for treating COVID-19 and documented adverse-effect risks.

**Relation to scenario:** `DIRECT`  
**Source:** https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/coronavirus-disease-%28covid-19%29-hydroxychloroquine

### Cochrane review — Publication bias in clinical trials

The review found positive trials were published more often and more quickly than negative or null trials.

**Relation to scenario:** `RELATED PATTERN`  
**Source:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8276556/

## Boundary

The documented cases illustrate analogous failure mechanisms. They do not establish that Koali would have prevented the event or guaranteed a different outcome.
