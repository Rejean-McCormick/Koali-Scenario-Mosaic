# The Heat Wave Is Here. Who Checks the People Most at Risk?

> An alert is not a response plan until somebody is responsible for reaching the person who may not survive it.

## The problem

Weather services can predict dangerous heat, health systems know many risk factors, municipalities know cooling resources, and community organizations know who is isolated. Those pieces rarely become one coordinated outreach operation.

## What breaks

- Fragmentation
- Resource Orchestration Failure
- Unclosed Loop
- Citizen Signal Ignored

## Who holds part of the picture

- public health
- clinicians
- community organizations
- affected people

## With Koali

Koali can turn a forecast into governed outreach: risk groups, authorized contact lists, assigned check-ins, transport or cooling options, escalation when contact fails, and a live status of unmet needs.

## Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

## What success looks like

- The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
- Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
- The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.

## Real-world parallels

### Extreme Heat and Human Mortality — BC Coroners Service

The review attributed 619 deaths to the 2021 heat event and emphasized identifying and supporting vulnerable people.

**Relation to scenario:** `DIRECT`  
**Source:** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/birth-adoption-death-marriage-and-divorce/deaths/coroners-service/death-review-panel/extreme_heat_death_review_panel_report.pdf

### Grenfell Tower Inquiry — Phase 2 Overview

The Inquiry found that residents’ concerns were repeatedly marginalized and earlier independent recommendations had produced little visible change.

**Relation to scenario:** `RELATED PATTERN`  
**Source:** https://www.grenfelltowerinquiry.org.uk/report/phase-2/overview.html

## Boundary

The documented cases illustrate analogous failure mechanisms. They do not establish that Koali would have prevented the event or guaranteed a different outcome.
