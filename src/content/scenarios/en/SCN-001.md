# A Child Is Missing. Every Minute Counts.

> The resources may already exist. The challenge is making the whole system act as one.

## The problem

A vulnerable child disappears. Family, school staff, patrol officers, investigators, search specialists, maps, behavioural profiles and external agencies may each hold part of the operational picture. The danger is not only lack of effort: search coverage, hypotheses, resource requests and unfinished obligations can fragment while time keeps passing.

## What breaks

- Fragmentation
- Resource Orchestration Failure
- Missing Escalation
- Unclosed Loop

## Who holds part of the picture

- family
- frontline responders
- specialists
- command

## With Koali

Koali maintains one evolving operational case: confirmed facts, uncertainty, search coverage, tasks, available and requested resources, decision gates, owners and escalation conditions. Unfinished requirements remain visible across shifts and agencies.

## Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

## What success looks like

- The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
- Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
- The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.

## Real-world parallels

### Enquête publique sur le décès de Maëlyne Lugez — Bureau du coroner du Québec

The inquiry examined the search response after a 13-year-old disappeared in Lévis and issued recommendations on search practices and specialized resources.

**Relation to scenario:** `DIRECT`  
**Source:** https://www.coroner.gouv.qc.ca/fileadmin/contenu/Enqu%C3%AAtes_publiques/Ma%C3%ABlyne_Lugez/2025-EP00301-9.pdf

### Turning the Tide Together — Mass Casualty Commission, Volume 5

The Commission documented critical-incident communication, public-warning and inter-agency coordination failures during the 2020 Nova Scotia mass casualty.

**Relation to scenario:** `STRONG ANALOGUE`  
**Source:** https://masscasualtycommission.ca/files/documents/Turning-the-Tide-Together-Volume-5-Policing.pdf

## Boundary

The documented cases illustrate analogous failure mechanisms. They do not establish that Koali would have prevented the event or guaranteed a different outcome.
