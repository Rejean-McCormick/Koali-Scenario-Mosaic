# Make the Unpopular Decision Reconstructible.

> A difficult decision may remain controversial; it should not become impossible to understand.

## The problem

Institutions sometimes must choose between bad options. A later controversy can reduce the history to a slogan: who voted for what. The evidence available at the time, legal constraints, rejected alternatives and dissent disappear.

## What breaks

- Opaque Decision
- Provenance Loss
- Context Loss
- Legitimacy Gap

## Who holds part of the picture

- citizens
- experts
- decision-makers
- affected groups

## With Koali

Koali publishes a decision record that links options, criteria, evidence, dissent, authority, final choice and implementation consequences. Public summaries remain connected to source material.

## Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

## What success looks like

- The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
- Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
- The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.

## Real-world parallels
