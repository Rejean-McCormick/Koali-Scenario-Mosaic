# The Professor's Best Course Disappears with the Account.

> A lifetime of teaching improvement should become a reusable asset, not an abandoned folder.

## The problem

A professor refines explanations, exercises, examples and sequencing for years. The material may live in a private LMS or personal drive; retirement, account closure or platform change can make that accumulated craft effectively disappear.

## What breaks

- Knowledge Attrition
- Content Silo
- Provenance Loss
- Reuse Failure

## Who holds part of the picture

- teachers
- learners
- institutions
- future cohorts

## With Koali

Koali turns teaching artifacts into living knowledge objects with attribution, sources, learning goals, versions, adaptations, evidence from practice and reuse permissions. Materials can evolve without losing authorship or why they were designed that way.

## Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

## What success looks like

- The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
- Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
- The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.

## Real-world parallels

### MIT OpenCourseWare

MIT openly publishes materials from more than 2,500 courses, showing that teaching knowledge can outlive a single class, account or instructor.

**Relation to scenario:** `REAL POSITIVE ANALOGUE`  
**Source:** https://ocw.mit.edu/about/

### UNESCO Recommendation on Open Educational Resources

UNESCO promotes open access, reuse, adaptation and redistribution of teaching and learning materials.

**Relation to scenario:** `STRONG ANALOGUE`  
**Source:** https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-open-educational-resources-oer

## Boundary

The documented cases illustrate analogous failure mechanisms. They do not establish that Koali would have prevented the event or guaranteed a different outcome.
