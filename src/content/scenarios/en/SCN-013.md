# The Failed Experiment Nobody Can Learn From.

> When only success becomes visible, everyone else can unknowingly pay to repeat the same failure.

## The problem

Negative results, abandoned prototypes and failed replications often contain high-value knowledge, but publication and incentive systems favour successful outcomes. The absence of visible failure distorts what future teams think is known.

## What breaks

- Publication Bias
- Memory Failure
- Provenance Loss
- Reuse Failure

## Who holds part of the picture

- researchers
- funders
- reviewers
- future teams

## With Koali

Koali preserves validated negative knowledge as first-class artifacts: method, provenance, uncertainty, why the team stopped, and what a future attempt would need to change.

## Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

## What success looks like

- The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
- Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
- The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.

## Real-world parallels

## Boundary

The documented cases illustrate analogous failure mechanisms. They do not establish that Koali would have prevented the event or guaranteed a different outcome.
