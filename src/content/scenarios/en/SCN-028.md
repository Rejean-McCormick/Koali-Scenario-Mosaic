# Another Disaster. Another Forgotten Report.

> Learning fails when recommendations become documents instead of obligations with owners, deadlines and evidence of closure.

## The problem

After a disaster, inquiries generate findings and recommendations. Years later, personnel change, attention moves on and implementation becomes hard to verify. A similar event exposes the same weak point.

## What breaks

- Memory Failure
- Unclosed Loop
- Recommendation Decay
- Institutional Amnesia

## Who holds part of the picture

- frontline practitioners
- domain experts
- decision-makers
- affected people

## With Koali

Koali converts recommendations into governed work: owner, scope, due date, dependencies, evidence required for closure, review date and link to the incident that created the obligation.

## Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

## What success looks like

- The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
- Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
- The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.

## Real-world parallels

### FERC/NERC — 2011 Southwest Cold Weather Event

FERC and NERC investigated the 2011 cold-weather outages and issued recommendations intended to prevent recurrence.

**Relation to scenario:** `DIRECT`  
**Source:** https://www.ferc.gov/sites/default/files/2020-04/08-16-11-report.pdf

### Grenfell Tower Inquiry — Phase 2 Overview

The Inquiry found that residents’ concerns were repeatedly marginalized and earlier independent recommendations had produced little visible change.

**Relation to scenario:** `DIRECT`  
**Source:** https://www.grenfelltowerinquiry.org.uk/report/phase-2/overview.html

## Boundary

The documented cases illustrate analogous failure mechanisms. They do not establish that Koali would have prevented the event or guaranteed a different outcome.
