# New Audit. New Names. Same Risk.

> An organization can produce repeated findings without building the memory that makes recurrence visible before the next failure.

## The problem

Audits identify weak oversight, incomplete risk reporting, procurement problems or missing controls. Management accepts recommendations. Years later a new programme exhibits the same pattern under different names.

## What breaks

- Memory Failure
- Recommendation Decay
- Fragmentation
- Unclosed Loop

## Who holds part of the picture

- public servants
- leaders
- auditors
- vendors

## With Koali

Koali links findings to reusable failure mechanisms and control patterns. New projects can be checked against prior lessons before the same risk becomes another audit paragraph.

## Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

## What success looks like

- The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
- Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
- The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.

## Real-world parallels
