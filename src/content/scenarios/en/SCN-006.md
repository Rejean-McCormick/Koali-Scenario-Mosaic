# The Warning Loses Its Meaning on the Way Up.

> A critical message can arrive at the top stripped of the uncertainty and reasoning that made it urgent.

## The problem

An expert raises a concern based on incomplete evidence. As the message passes through layers, uncertainty is compressed into a yes/no conclusion and the request for more information becomes an optional technical preference.

## What breaks

- Context Loss
- Signal Dilution
- Missing Escalation
- False Certainty

## Who holds part of the picture

- frontline practitioners
- domain experts
- decision-makers
- affected people

## With Koali

Koali makes the warning portable without flattening it: claim, evidence, source, assumptions, uncertainty, requested action, dissent and escalation status travel together. Independent warnings can be recognized as converging signals.

## Human authority

Authorized humans and institutions retain their existing legal, professional and democratic authority. Koali structures knowledge, coordination and traceability; it does not take sovereign or professional decisions.

## What success looks like

- The relevant state remains visible across handoffs and time.
- Responsibilities, unresolved questions and escalation paths are explicit.
- The outcome produces reusable memory for the next comparable case.

## Real-world parallels

### Columbia Accident Investigation Board Report

The CAIB found ineffective communication, three independent imagery requests, reliance on informal channels and inadequate discussion of assumptions and uncertainty.

**Relation to scenario:** `DIRECT`  
**Source:** https://history2.nasa.gov/columbia/reports/CAIBreportv1.pdf

### Auditor General of Canada — Phoenix example

The Auditor General reiterated that Phoenix executives prioritized schedule and budget over critical elements and did not provide senior officials a complete picture of project risks.

**Relation to scenario:** `STRONG ANALOGUE`  
**Source:** https://www.oag-bvg.gc.ca/internet/docs/parl_oag_202310_07_e.pdf

## Boundary

The documented cases illustrate analogous failure mechanisms. They do not establish that Koali would have prevented the event or guaranteed a different outcome.

## Koali loop

`signals → shared meaning → evidence → choice → responsibility → action → verification → memory → better knowledge`
